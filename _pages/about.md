I am a researcher in quantum information and a software developer based in Paris, working at Inria.

My research focuses on secure and verifiable quantum computation, quantum communication, and the development of software that makes quantum-information ideas easier to explore and use.

My work spans theoretical foundations and practical research infrastructure, including delegated quantum computation, measurement-based quantum computation, and quantum-state certification.

This site collects my [publications](/publications/), [software projects](/software/), talks, and [CV](/cv/). Code and research materials are available on [GitHub](https://github.com/mgarnier59), with publication records also available through [Google Scholar]({{ site.author.googlescholar }}) and [arXiv]({{ site.author.arxiv }}).
